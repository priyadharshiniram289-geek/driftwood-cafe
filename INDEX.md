## How the menu was merged

The old `menu.csv` and `copy of menu.csv` disagreed. The merged `menu/menu.csv` resolves them like this:

| Item | Old menu.csv | Old copy | Now | Why |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Brown bread (loaf) | €4.00 | €4.50 | **€4.50** | Monday notes: "brown bread price going to 4.50" |
| Flat white | €3.60 | €3.80 | **€3.60** | Owner confirmed €3.60 |
| Carrot cake | (missing) | €4.20 | **€4.20, included** | Only in the newer copy. Check it's still on sale. |

All other items had the same price in both files.
